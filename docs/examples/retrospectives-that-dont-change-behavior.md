# Worked Example — Retrospectives That Don't Change Behavior

A worked application of [Grandmaster Mode](../grandmaster-mode.md).

## Observation

A team holds a retrospective at the end of every cycle. People are candid and
action items are written down, but the same problems return cycle after cycle.

This evidence extension supports the canonical **♟️ Position Summary**.

## Pattern

The ritual creates reflection, but the reflection does not transfer into the next
cycle. Signal is produced and then dropped.

This reasoning extension supports the canonical **♟️ Position Summary**.

## ♟️ Position Summary

In the observed team, recurring problems persist across cycles despite candid
retrospectives and recorded action items. The observation supports a failure to
carry retrospective learning into this team's next cycle; it does not establish
that the same failure occurs in other contexts.

## ♜ Candidate Abstractions

- A ritual is not learning.
- Reflection is not transfer.
- A lesson that is not carried forward does not improve future modeling.

## 🧠 Transfer Analysis

### Observed transfer evidence

- Within the observed team, the same problems return in later cycles after
  retrospectives and action items.
- No observations from another team or another learning context are included,
  so cross-context transfer remains untested.

### Transfer hypotheses

- **Other teams:** the pattern may recur when retrospective lessons are not
  carried into later cycles.
- **Individual learning:** reflection may fail to change later practice without
  a carry-forward mechanism.
- **Documentation review:** review findings may fail to improve later documents
  when they are not carried forward.
- **Model evaluation comparison:** evaluation insights may fail to improve later
  modeling when they are not carried forward and compared with subsequent
  observations.

These are hypotheses for future observation, not evidence that transfer has
occurred in those contexts.

## 🏁 Meta-Lesson

A learning ritual without closure produces reflection, not transfer. The reusable
primitive is:

```text
Reflection without carry-forward does not improve future modeling.
```
