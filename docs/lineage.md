# Historical Context and Lineage

MindShift did not begin as a finished idea. It emerged from years of exploration
that moved through successive generations of thinking about how understanding
deepens:

```text
Information
  → Learning
    → Reflection
      → Recursive Reflection
        → Networked Reflection
          → Self-Generating Frameworks
```

Each generation added a turn of the loop:

- **Information** — accumulation of facts and signal.
- **Learning** — turning information into updated behavior.
- **Reflection** — examining the learning, not just the result.
- **Recursive Reflection** — reflecting on the reflection; learning to learn.
- **Networked Reflection** — reflection distributed across many learners and
  contexts.
- **Self-Generating Frameworks** — structures that produce and refine their own
  successors.

## The Recurring Invariant

Across all generations, one thing held constant:

> Learning becomes intelligence when it loops back on itself.

This is the invariant the repository exists to preserve and refine. Every
addition to MindShift should be checked against it: does this strengthen the loop
that turns learning back on itself, or merely add information beside it?

## Why Preserve the Lineage

A lineage records not only conclusions but the path that produced them — which is
itself a learning artifact. Keeping the lineage visible lets future cycles see
where ideas came from, why earlier forms were superseded, and what invariant
survived every revision. The history is part of the system's memory.
